Delete your HeadsUp Scouting account

You can delete your account in the app or request deletion by email without installing the app or signing in.

Français

Request deletion by email

  1. Email privacy@headsupscouting.com. The link only opens a draft. If it doesn't open, copy the address and start a new message in your email app or webmail.
  2. Include the email address associated with your account and state clearly that you want to delete your HeadsUp Scouting account.
  3. Send the message. An unsent draft is not a deletion request.

Do not send sensitive sign-in or security information, identity documents, or player or scouting records.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to HeadsUp Scouting and tap the gear icon in the bottom-right corner to open Settings.
  2. Select Delete account.
  3. In the confirmation dialog, press and hold the delete button to confirm.

If you cancel before confirming, deletion won't start. If the app shows an error, don't assume deletion is complete. Try again or use the email option above.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

What deletion changes

When in-app deletion succeeds, the app signs you out. The service disables the account, replaces or clears profile information, and removes tokens used to renew sessions. This does not mean all data linked to the account is erased.
