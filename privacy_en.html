Privacy Policy

V0.1  ·  Last updated: June 19, 2026  ·  Effective since: June 19, 2026

This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights. Reading or using the service does not replace any separate consent required by law.

1. Data we collect

Information processed through the service includes:

  • Account information: information supplied when requesting access or using an account, such as your name and email address.
  • Scouting records: notes, viewings, and structured evaluations, as well as player profile information such as names, dates of birth, height, weight, and positions.
  • Operational information: hosting and monitoring providers may process operational or diagnostic data.

The mobile application also caches account, watchlist, viewing, and note-related data in a local SQLite database to support offline use.

2. How we use your data

The data collected is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the service.
  • Send you communications related to your account.
  • Generate structured evaluations from scouting note comments using Azure OpenAI.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Sharing with third parties

Information may be shared with:

  • Service providers: providers supporting the service, including hosting and monitoring providers. Note comments are sent to Azure OpenAI to generate evaluations.
  • Legal authorities: where required by applicable law or court order.

Our Azure AI resource is located in Canada Central, but its production deployment uses Global Standard. For evaluation generation, prompts containing note comments and the generated responses may be processed in any geography where the model is deployed, including outside Quebec and Canada; the resource location does not confine that processing to Canada. See Microsoft's data privacy guidance.

Separately from generating evaluations, Microsoft uses automated abuse monitoring. According to Microsoft, prompts and responses flagged for potential abuse may be sampled for automated review and, in some cases, reviewed by authorized Microsoft personnel. See Microsoft's abuse monitoring guidance.

Scouts should review AI-generated evaluations before relying on them.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

For questions about cookies or similar technologies, contact us at privacy@headsupscouting.com. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data retention

We retain information as needed to provide the service and meet applicable legal obligations. To request account or personal data deletion, contact privacy@headsupscouting.com. We will explain the process and applicable retention requirements when responding to your request.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: request the deletion of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured and machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to certain processing activities, particularly for marketing purposes.
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@headsupscouting.com. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You may also contact Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information about a privacy concern or available recourse, as applicable.

7. Security

We take steps to protect information used by the service, but no system is completely secure. In the event of an incident affecting your data, we will act in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

8. Minors

Accounts are intended for adult, authorized scouts. Scouting records may include information about players who are minors. Scouts must have authority to enter player information and enter only information necessary for scouting. Players, parents, or guardians may contact privacy@headsupscouting.com with privacy questions or requests concerning player information. Requests will be considered under applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. In the event of a significant change, we will notify you by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.

We will obtain separate consent where required by applicable law.

10. Contact us

For any questions regarding this policy or your personal data:
