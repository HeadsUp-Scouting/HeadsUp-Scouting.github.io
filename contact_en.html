Frequently asked questions

Does HeadsUp evaluate players automatically?

HeadsUp can analyze notes and observations entered by the scout to identify trends and structure the information. However, evaluations remain based on the scout's observations.

Does HeadsUp replace the scout's judgment?

No. HeadsUp structures and puts the scout's observations into context, but the judgment remains entirely with the professional. The goal is to make information easier to review and use, not to make decisions on their behalf.

How can I get support or share feedback?

You can contact us directly through the contact form. Every message is read and used to improve the product.
